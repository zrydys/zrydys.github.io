
English is the official FSM government and commerce language. Eight major indigenous languages are: Yapese, Ulithian, Woleaians, Chuukese, Pohnpeians, Kosraeans, Nukuoro, Kapingamarangi. Many elderly people are fluent in Japanese.


### **Language Promotion & Roadmap**

**Promoted Languages:**

- **Local languages** (e.g., Pohnpeian, Kosraean, Yapese, Chuukese, Ulithian, Woleian, Mokilese, Nukuoran, Kapingamarangian) are prioritized  
- **English** is the official language for national and international communication.
- **Other international languages** (Japanese, Chinese, Korean) ..

**Roadmap & Goals:**

- **Short-term:** Develop reference grammars, dictionaries, and instructional materials for local languages; improve English teaching methods and materials.
- **Long-term:** Achieve multilingualism (local language + English + one other international language) for all students; integrate language learning with economic sectors (agriculture, marine, tourism).
- **Institutional Support:** Establish a National Language and Cultural Institute (NLCI) to coordinate research, materials development, and teacher training.
